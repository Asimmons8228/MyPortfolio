About Me

Hey there! I'm Aaron Simmons, an entrepreneur, Web Development aficionado, and Muay Thai enthusiast calling the vibrant streets of New York City home. Picture me as your friendly neighborhood coder with a knack for turning lines of code into captivating online experiences. Whether it's JavaScript, React.js, Python, or a sprinkle of HTML and CSS magic, I'm all about bringing innovative ideas to life on the web.

I'm a Solutions Architect focused on helping customers turn complex technical requirements into practical, successful implementations. At OpenAsset, I lead end-to-end implementations for Architecture, Engineering, and Construction clients, partnering with Sales, customer IT teams, executives, Product, Engineering, and Support.

My work sits at the intersection of customer success and hands-on technical problem solving. I work with APIs, ERP integrations, SSO, Python, data transformation, and automation—and I enjoy finding solutions when the path forward isn't obvious.

Before moving into Solutions Architecture, I worked as a Support Engineer at OpenAsset, where I closed the highest volume of tickets during my first fiscal year while maintaining a 98% CSAT score. Earlier in my career, I worked in software development, technical recruiting, and business operations.
